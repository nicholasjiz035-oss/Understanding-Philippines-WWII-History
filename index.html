Introduction

The Philippines War started on December 8th, 1941, a day after the bombing of Pearl Harbor. General Douglas MacArthur led the Far Eastern Air Force stationed in the Philippines, which posed a significant hurdle to Japanese control over the Pacific islands. During WWII, the Philippine Islands posed a risk to the Japanese since American forces could easily launch air assaults from the islands. Japan invading the Philippines was a logistical plan designed to capture the airfields and the fueling capacities held by the United States (Gruodis-Gimbel, 2023).

Purpose and the Importance of this Study

World War II in the Philippines is important to know and understand because it reminds us of the sacrifices, courage, and suffering experienced by Filipinos during the war. Learning about this period helps us appreciate our freedom, respect our history, and understand the importance of peace. As the younger generation, we have the responsibility to preserve these historical lessons so they will not be forgotten.
